## Internship at Bosch Japan

I joined [Bosch Japan](https://www.linkedin.com/company/bosch-japan/) as an intern in the Engineering Customer Powertrain Japan department, within the Two-Wheeler and Powersports division. My work supported customer engineering for two-wheeler powertrain components, with a focus on electric motors and control units.

My responsibilities included creating project-complexity matrices, maintaining project overview documentation and department guidelines, supporting sample flashing with INCA, carrying out vehicle measurements and evaluating results with self-created scripts, and conducting market studies for electrified powertrain business opportunities.

## Intern Networking Day at Bosch

Bosch Japan holds an Intern Networking Day every three months, giving interns an opportunity to build networks, learn more about Bosch, and explore a shared topic. I joined an event at the Yokohama headquarters focused on diversity and Christmas.
