## Robotics Manufacturing and Assembly Internship

I joined Model T Operations as an hourly intern at its Harumi headquarters in Tokyo. The internship focused on duties related to the manufacturing and assembly of robots and peripheral equipment.

This role provided practical experience in a robotics-focused work environment and supported the production and integration of robotic systems.

### Additional Information

**Company**: Model T Operations Corporation.  
**Location**: Harumi, Tokyo, Japan.  
**Internship period**: April 22, 2024 - August 31, 2024.  
**Focus**: Manufacturing and assembly of robots and peripheral equipment.
